About Rise Axis

Clarity, Direction, Results — the principles that drive everything we do for farmers across East Africa.

1000+ Customers served

— Our Story

Rooted in Agriculture, Driven by Innovation

Rise Axis Solutions Ltd was founded on a simple but powerful belief: that farmers deserve access to world-class agri-inputs, honest advice, and reliable supply — all in one place.

Today, we stand as one of East Africa's most trusted agri-input suppliers, serving the floriculture, horticulture, and general farming sectors with a comprehensive portfolio of scientifically proven products.

From crop protection chemicals and specialty fertilizers to irrigation systems, greenhouse sheeting, and animal health products, our range is curated to meet the real, everyday needs of farmers on the ground.
