Our Story

Rooted in Agriculture, Driven by Innovation

Rise Axis Solutions Ltd was founded on a simple but powerful belief: that farmers deserve access to world-class agri-inputs, honest advice, and reliable supply all in one place.

Today, we stand as one of East Africa's most trusted agri-input suppliers, serving livestock keepers, horticulture, and general farming sectors with a comprehensive portfolio of scientifically proven products.

From feed additives and premixes to veterinary products and injectables, our range is curated to meet the real, everyday needs of farmers on the ground.

Mission, Vision & Values

The mission, vision, and values that guide every decision we make.

Our Mission

Our Vision
