Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often.

Do you deliver outside Nairobi?
Yes — we deliver across Kenya and the wider East African region. Delivery timelines and costs depend on your location and order size. Reach out to our sales team for a quote.
Can I request a product catalogue?
Absolutely. Send us a message through the form above and we'll email you the latest catalogue.
Do you offer technical support for farmers?
Yes. Our agronomy team provides field support, product guidance, and tailored recommendations. Contact agronomy@riseaxis.co.ke to schedule a consultation.
How do I become a distributor or partner?
We're always open to new partnerships. Send us a message using the form above with the subject "Partnership" and our team will get back to you.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept bank transfers, M-Pesa, and major cards for online orders. For bulk purchases, our sales team can arrange invoicing.
